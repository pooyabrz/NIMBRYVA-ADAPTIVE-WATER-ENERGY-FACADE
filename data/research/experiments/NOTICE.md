# Independently measured numerical data

`dew-data.xlsx` and numerical derivatives retain **CC BY-NC 4.0**, attributed to Haechler et al. (2021), ETH Zurich, DOI 10.3929/ethz-b-000469811. Commercial permission is not inferred from the code licence.

Wind-driven-rain event and error-table numerical data are attributed to Kubilay, Derome, Blocken and Carmeliet, ETH Zurich / Empa. Their download page permits use with source acknowledgement. The original uncertainty tables are retained; invalid ETOT values are not converted to zero uncertainty.

PV module performance matrices are attributed to Sandia National Laboratories / PVPMC, measured module data, 2019. They concern the listed actual modules, not the unknown internal construction of the proposed facade PV panel.

`SOURCES.json` records source links and SHA-256 values. No journal full text is included. Model predictions and fitted coefficients are labelled separately from measurements; calibration and validation run/event/condition membership is explicit in the normalized files.
